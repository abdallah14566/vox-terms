VOX - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data VOX ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Data We Collect

The Bot collects and stores only the minimum data required to function properly:

  • User IDs: To identify users (ticket ownership, application status, blacklist system).
  • Guild IDs: To store server-specific settings (panels, logs, roles, language).
  • Channel IDs: To store channel-specific settings (panel channels, log channels).
  • Role IDs: To determine support team permissions.
  • Ticket Data: Ticket status, claim status, open time, and configuration.
  • Application Data: User answers to application questions (stored in log channels).
  • Message Content: Only when generating a transcript, and only for messages inside ticket channels.

We do NOT collect:

  • Passwords or login credentials.
  • Payment information.
  • Private messages (DMs) outside the ticket system.
  • Voice data or audio recordings.

2. How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used exclusively for:

  • Operating the Bot's core features (tickets, applications, logs).
  • Storing server-specific settings (language, ticket limits).
  • Generating transcripts when tickets are closed.
  • Technical maintenance and bug fixing.
  • Enforcing blacklists and anti-abuse systems.

We never:

  • Sell your data.
  • Share your data with third parties.
  • Use your data for advertising.
  • Access your data for personal reasons.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored in a local database on the server hosting the Bot.
  • We take reasonable measures to protect data from unauthorized access.
  • Data is automatically deleted when a ticket is closed or when the Bot is removed from a server.

4. Data Retention

  • Ticket data: Deleted when a ticket is closed or when the Bot leaves the server.
  • Application data: Retained in log channels for administrative review.
  • Guild settings: Retained until the Bot is removed or settings are reset.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we store about you or your server.
  • Request deletion of all data associated with your account or server.
  • Opt-out of any non-essential data collection.

To exercise these rights, contact us via:

  • Support Server: https://discord.gg/jaJMdP4DBH
  • Email: vox.support13@gmail.com

All valid requests will be processed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

7. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates on Discord and may interact with:

  • Discord API (governed by Discord's Privacy Policy).
  • Hosting providers (for uptime and database storage).

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced in our support server. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
